Common Questions About Om Cloud Consulting

Everything you need to know about partnering with our Salesforce architect-led engineering boutique.

Unlike traditional giant IT vendors where your project is handed off to junior developers, Om Cloud Consulting operates on an architect-led delivery model. Rahul Sharma, Yash Agrawal, Kirtesh Khandelwal, and Mohnish Tak personally architect and oversee every solution. We guarantee 100% test-driven Apex/LWC code, zero technical debt, and direct architect communication without intermediary account managers.
Agentforce is Salesforce's next-generation autonomous AI platform. Rather than simple chatbots that only generate text, Agentforce uses the Atlas Reasoning Engine to reason, retrieve enterprise knowledge via Data Cloud RAG, and autonomously execute multi-step business actions (e.g. loan pre-qualification, case deflection, scheduling). OCC configures enterprise guardrails, PII masking, and custom Invocable Apex actions with built-in rollback safeguards.
nCino is the worldwide leader in cloud banking built natively on Salesforce. OCC specializes in end-to-end commercial lending origination, automated financial statement Spreading, covenant tracking, and seamless core banking integrations (FIS, Fiserv, Jack Henry). Our implementations reduce commercial loan cycle times by up to 68% while guaranteeing strict federal regulatory compliance.
Data 360 ingests high-velocity streaming data from POS, mobile telemetry, webhooks, and external enterprise warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery, AWS S3) via Zero-Copy federation. OCC designs deterministic Identity Resolution rules that unite fragmented records into a unified individual profile, streaming real-time Calculated Insights onto CRM record pages and grounding AI agents with trusted context.
